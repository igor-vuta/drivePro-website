# Drive Pro WhatsApp messages — private review draft

These are complete message drafts, not an implemented quote flow or sent messages. Replace each bracketed answer with the visitor's actual answer; when omitted, use the indicated unknown text. Show the full result for editing before opening WhatsApp. The visitor must review the text and press Send in WhatsApp. Photos or video can be sent later in WhatsApp; no website upload is proposed. See [CLAIMS_AND_REVIEW.md](CLAIMS_AND_REVIEW.md) for review dependencies.

Работа: [ответ / хочу обсудить]

### English (`en`)

```text
Hello! I would like an individual quote for excavator work with your operator in Almaty. I am not requesting an excavator without your operator.
Job: [answer / to discuss]
Location: [area, address or landmark / I will confirm later]
Date: [preferred date and flexibility / to discuss]
Volume or dimensions: [m³ or length × width × depth in metres / I do not know]
Access and restrictions: [width, turns, height, slope, surface or other details / needs checking]
Ground and site conditions: [answer, including known underground services / needs checking]
Spoil removal or loading: [what I would like to discuss / to discuss]
Photos or video: [I can send them later / none yet]
Please advise what equipment may suit the job, whether the work is possible and what an individual quote would be.
```

The last sentence asks for assessment; it does not assert that a machine, date, removal service or quote is available. The exact site location, service area beyond Almaty, equipment suitability and any commercial terms still need confirmation.
